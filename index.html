Glyph Studio privacy policy

Effective 1 October 2026

Glyph Studio is made by MaxMax300 ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the app does with information on your phone.

The short version

Glyph Studio does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no internet access. Everything it does happens on your phone.

Your designs

Designs you draw or import are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. They leave the phone only if you choose to share or back them up, using Android's share sheet or file picker, and they go only where you send them. Android may include app data in your own Google device backup if backups are turned on in your phone's settings. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account, not by me.

Photos, GIFs and videos

When you import one, you pick it with Android's picker. The app reads it only to turn it into LED frames, and does not keep or send the original.

Microphone and phone audio (Sound tab)

The sound visualiser listens to the microphone, or to audio playing on the phone, only while you have it switched on, and a notification shows while it runs. Sound is turned into light levels in memory, as it happens. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Notification access (Studio Notifications toy)

If you turn on notification access, the app counts how many notifications you have so the toy can show the number. It does not read, store or send their content, senders or any other details. You can turn this off at any time in Android's notification access settings.

Glyph Matrix

The app uses the Nothing Glyph Matrix developer kit to light the LEDs on the back of the phone. No personal data is sent to Nothing through it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
